# Project Overview

## What is this project?
The Face Recognition-Based Attendance System is an advanced workforce management platform. It replaces traditional RFID cards, passwords, and manual sign-in sheets with frictionless biometric facial recognition.

## Why was it created?
Traditional attendance systems are fraught with inefficiencies. RFID cards can be shared or forgotten (buddy-punching), manual registers are prone to human error and deliberate forgery, and fingerprint scanners raise hygiene concerns. Furthermore, historical attendance logs are frequently manipulated retroactively by managers or DBAs, destroying the integrity of payroll and compliance audits.

## What problem does it solve?
1. **Time Theft**: Eliminates buddy punching entirely. You cannot clock in for someone else.
2. **Audit Integrity**: By anchoring attendance hashes to the Ethereum blockchain, not even a SuperAdmin with direct SQL database access can silently alter an attendance record without breaking the cryptographically verified audit trail.
3. **Frictionless Experience**: Employees walk up to an edge device (or use their web portal), their face is recognized in under 2 seconds, and their shift is logged automatically.
4. **Employee Wellness**: By passively detecting macro-level emotion trends across an organization, HR can intervene when burnout risk metrics trigger alerts.

## Industry Relevance
With the shift toward hybrid work environments and zero-trust architectures, biometrics are becoming the gold standard for identity verification. Integrating this with Web3 distributed ledgers places this platform at the bleeding edge of enterprise HRTech.

## Business Value
* **Direct Cost Savings**: Recoups millions lost to time theft and buddy punching.
* **Compliance**: Provides undeniable mathematical proof of presence for highly regulated industries (healthcare, defense, construction).
* **Operational Efficiency**: Automates the pipeline from the front door directly to the payroll system, eliminating manual data entry.

## Expected Outcomes
* 99.9% reduction in fraudulent check-ins.
* < 2.5 second median check-in time per employee.
* Complete real-time visibility into workforce distribution across multiple corporate campuses.
